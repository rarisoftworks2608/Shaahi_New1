Good to know

Questions We Are Often Asked.

Short answers to the things most teams want to know before they get in touch.

What does Shaahi Creations do?

Shaahi Creations is a corporate event management company founded in 2018, headquartered in Hyderabad with offices in Pune and Mumbai. We design and deliver conferences, leadership summits, annual days, family days, product launches, dealer meets, brand activations, team offsites and destination corporate events across India.

Where does Shaahi Creations operate?

We operate PAN India and have executed more than 1500 events across 25+ cities, including Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai, Ahmedabad, Jaipur and Indore, as well as destination locations such as Goa, Udaipur, Rishikesh, Coorg, Jim Corbett and Kabini.

What size of events can Shaahi Creations manage?

We have delivered events from intimate leadership retreats of 100 guests to large-format celebrations for audiences of 4000. Our largest single event managed to date hosted 4000 attendees.

Which industries does Shaahi Creations work with?

We work across IT and technology, GCCs, healthcare, pharma, retail, hospitality, government, manufacturing, automobile, education, telecom and BFSI. Clients include Microsoft, NVIDIA, Amazon, Marriott, Porsche, Capgemini, Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Welspun and the World Health Organization.

How do I request a proposal?

Share your event objective, approximate audience size, preferred dates and city through the contact form, or write to events@shaahicreations.com. We typically respond within 24 hours during business hours, Monday to Saturday.
