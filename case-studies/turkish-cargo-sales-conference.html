The objective

A sales conference for Turkish Cargo’s PAN India leadership and sales team, staged as a Hyderabad 10th anniversary special event. It had to mark a milestone for the brand and still work as a business meeting for 600+ attendees.

How we approached it

  • High-impact conference production built around a branded anniversary stage.
  • Brand identity carried through the stage, backdrops and venue at Trident, Hyderabad.
  • Guest experience and logistics for a leadership and sales team arriving from across India.
  • Entertainment and engagement programmed alongside the business sessions.

The outcome

A 600+ attendee conference that gave the anniversary the presence of a brand milestone while keeping the focus of a sales meeting.

What we delivered

  • PAN India leadership & sales conference
  • High-impact conference production
  • Branding, guest experience & logistics
  • Entertainment & engagement
